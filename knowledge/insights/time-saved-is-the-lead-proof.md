# Time saved is the lead proof metric

**Status:** emerging (one source)

Most metrics on the customer page are hours or days saved, more than dollars saved. See [Notion](../customers/notion.md), [Barry's](../customers/barrys.md), [Vanta](../customers/vanta.md). [source: https://ramp.com/customers]

Implication: lead outbound with hours back unless the account's pain is money. Promote to validated after a second source, such as call transcripts.
