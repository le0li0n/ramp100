# Controller

## Motion
[sales-led] champion

## Role
Runs accounting and the month-end close day to day.

## Goals
Close faster, code transactions correctly, clean audit trail, fewer manual reconciliations.

## Pains
Chasing receipts, miscoded spend, reimbursement backlog, ERP sync errors.

## Triggers
Close takes more than a week, a new entity, an audit, a missed accrual.

## Objections
Will it sync to our ERP, who codes transactions, what happens to history, implementation effort.

## Language
close, coding, sync, GL, accruals, receipts

## How they evaluate
Hands-on demo, ERP integration test, customer reference in same ERP.
