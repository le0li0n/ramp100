# CFO / VP Finance

## Motion
[sales-led] economic buyer

## Role
Owns the finance function and budget; signs off on platform changes.

## Goals
Control spend, shorten the close, give the board clean numbers, reduce headcount growth in finance.

## Pains
Surprise spend, slow close, tool sprawl across cards, AP and expenses.

## Triggers
New in role, funding round, audit finding, ERP project.

## Objections
Switching risk, security and compliance, 'we already have a card', change fatigue.

## Language
close, controls, forecast, visibility, audit-ready

## How they evaluate
Peer references, a business case with hours and dollars saved, security review passed.
