# Head of AP / AP Manager

## Motion
[sales-led] champion for AP and procurement

## Role
Owns bill intake, approval and payment.

## Goals
Pay on time, capture discounts, stop duplicate and fraudulent payments, reduce manual entry.

## Pains
Invoices by email and paper, approval chasing, vendor onboarding, 1099 filing.

## Triggers
Volume growth, AP clerk hire, a payment error, a vendor dispute.

## Objections
Vendor migration pain, approval workflow fit, payment methods vendors will accept.

## Language
invoice, PO, approval, three-way match, vendor, 1099

## How they evaluate
Process walkthrough on their real invoices, approval-flow fit, vendor-payment coverage.
