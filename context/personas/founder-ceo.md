# Founder / CEO at a small company

## Motion
[self-serve] buyer and user

## Role
Runs the company and often the finance function too.

## Goals
Give the team cards fast, keep spend visible, avoid hiring finance staff early.

## Pains
No time for finance admin, receipts, surprise charges, a personal guarantee on credit.

## Triggers
Hiring the first employees, first outside investor, outgrowing a founder's personal card.

## Objections
Do I need this yet, is it really free, what is the catch, will it work with my bookkeeper's tool.

## Language
free, cards, receipts, bookkeeping, budget

## How they evaluate
Self-serve signup, price on the page, accounting sync in the first week.
