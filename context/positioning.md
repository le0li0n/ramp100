# Positioning

## Category claim
"Finance automation platform": one platform for cards, spend, AP and close, run by AI agents. [assumption, adapted from the homepage line "One platform for all of finance" at https://ramp.com]

## Value proposition
Finance teams spend less, close faster and keep control, because spend is controlled at the point of purchase and the back office is automated. [assumption]

## Messaging pillars
1. **Save time.** Faster close, automatic coding, faster bill and reimbursement processing. Proof: [customers](../knowledge/customers/).
2. **Save money.** Spend controls and savings insights. [source: https://ramp.com/customers story themes]
3. **One platform.** Cards, expenses, AP, procurement, travel and banking in one system. [source: https://ramp.com]
4. **Agents do the work.** Policy Agents and Ramp Intelligence handle reviews and coding. [source: https://ramp.com]
5. **Switch fast.** "Switch in days, not months." [source: https://ramp.com]
