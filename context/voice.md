# Voice

Plain, specific, confident, and a little dry. Lead with the outcome and a number when one exists. Write like a sharp finance peer, not a vendor. [assumption, adapted from the homepage tone at https://ramp.com]

## Words we use
close, spend, controls, policy, savings, hours back, switch, one platform

## Words we avoid
revolutionary, game-changing, seamless, synergy, leverage (as a verb), "unlock", "empower", "in today's fast-paced world"

## Before and after

**1. Outbound opener**
- Before: "I wanted to reach out to introduce Ramp, a revolutionary spend management solution that can empower your finance team."
- After: "Saw you hired a new Controller in September. Teams at your size usually feel month-end close first. Notion's finance team got hours back every month ([proof](../knowledge/customers/notion.md)). Worth 15 minutes?"

**2. Objection reply (we already use another card)**
- Before: "Ramp is better than your current provider and offers more features."
- After: "Fair. Most teams we talk to aren't unhappy with the card, it's the coding and approvals behind it. Switching takes days, and your card can stay live until you cut over."

**3. Post hook**
- Before: "We're excited to announce our latest AI-powered capabilities for finance professionals."
- After: "Your policy lives in a PDF nobody reads. An agent can read it for every transaction. Here's what that caught in week one."
