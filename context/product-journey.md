# Product journey `[self-serve]`

For teams that sign up without talking to sales. [source: Free and Plus plans at https://ramp.com/pricing]

## Stages
1. **Signup:** "Get started for free" on ramp.com.
2. **First card issued:** the first virtual or physical card.
3. **Activation:** accounting system connected and cards with spend on at least three users within 30 days. [assumption]
4. **Habit:** receipts and coding handled in Ramp, a bill paid through Ramp.
5. **Expansion:** upgrade to Plus, add Procurement or AR, or move to sales-assisted Enterprise.

## Hand-off to sales
Route a Free or Plus account to an AE when it matches [Tier 1 or 2](icp.md) or sends a [self-serve signal](signals.md). Threshold values: TODO from Ramp product data.
