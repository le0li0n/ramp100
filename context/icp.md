# ICP `[sales-led]`

Company-size labels follow the Ramp site filters (SMB, Mid-size, Enterprise). Employee ranges below are assumptions. [assumption]

## Tiers

| Tier | Who | Motion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mid-size: 200 to 1,000 employees, growing, finance team of 5 to 25, outgrowing spreadsheets or a legacy card | Mid-Market AE |
| 2 | Enterprise: 1,000+ employees or multi-entity and international, ERP such as Workday, Oracle or NetSuite | Large Accounts AE |
| 3 | SMB: under 200 employees | Self-serve first; see [product journey](product-journey.md) |

## Fit filters
- Firmographic: US or one of the 30+ currency markets Ramp supports. [source: https://ramp.com/pricing]
- Technographic: ERP or accounting system Ramp integrates with (QuickBooks, NetSuite, Xero, Sage Intacct, Workday, Oracle). TODO: confirm the full list.
- Pain: slow month-end close, manual bill coding, reimbursement backlog, no spend controls. [source: story themes at https://ramp.com/customers]

## Anti-ICP
- Under 10 employees with almost no card or bill volume (send to self-serve, not an AE).
- Companies in a multi-year contract with no exit window. [assumption]

## Scoring rubric (0 to 100)

| Dimension | Weight | Full marks when |
|---|---|---|
| Size and growth | 25 | Tier 1 or 2 size, headcount up 20% or more in 12 months |
| Finance pain | 20 | Public signal of close, AP or reimbursement pain |
| Stack fit | 15 | Supported ERP or accounting tool |
| Trigger and timing | 20 | A [signal](signals.md) in the last 90 days |
| Spend profile | 10 | High card or bill volume, multi-entity, international |
| Logo and strategic value | 10 | Recognizable brand or a lookalike of a named customer |

Score bands: 80+ prioritize now, 60 to 79 sequence, under 60 nurture or self-serve.
