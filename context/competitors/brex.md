# Brex

## Overview
Corporate card and spend management vendor, generally positioned as the sales-led option for startups and mid-market. [source: WebSearch roundup, vendor-run comparison pages]

## Where they win
High-limit corporate cards; underwriting without personal guarantees, which appeals to startups. [source: WebSearch roundup]

## Where Ramp wins
Cards, expenses and AP in one platform; cashback; procurement. Claim comes from Ramp's own comparison blog, so it is vendor-biased. [source: https://ramp.com/blog/navan-vs-brex-vs-ramp]

## Pricing posture
Entry plan shown as free 'Essentials' on Ramp's page; G2 shows 'Premium' with 'Contact us'. Sources conflict. TODO: confirm on brex.com.

## Talk track
Don't attack the card. Ask what happens after the swipe: coding, approvals, bill pay. Then compare the whole workflow.
