# Signals and plays

Each signal maps to a play. `[sales-led]` unless marked. Signals are assumptions until validated against closed-won data. [assumption]

| Signal | Why it matters | Play |
|---|---|---|
| New CFO, Controller or VP Finance hired | New leaders re-evaluate tools in their first 90 days | `account-research` then `outbound-draft`, lead with close speed |
| Funding round announced | New spend and new controls needed | `outbound-draft`, lead with spend controls |
| Hiring AP clerk, accountant or finance ops | Manual work is growing | `outbound-draft`, lead with AP automation |
| ERP migration or new entity | Integration decisions are being made | `call-prep` with the integration story |
| Competitor contract renewal window (if known) | Switching window | `outbound-draft`, lead with "switch in days" |
| Headcount up 30% or more in a year | Reimbursement and card volume rise | `icp-score-list` to prioritize |
| `[self-serve]` Free account activates and invites 5+ users | Outgrowing Free | `pql-review` |
| `[self-serve]` Free account asks for Procurement, AR or enterprise integrations | Needs a paid or sales-assisted plan | `expansion-brief` |
